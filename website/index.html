1 what it is

your google drive in finder, with terabytes of cloud space.

it pins right in finder favorites and mounts as a native virtual disk. your files look and open like normal, in finder, terminal, and the apps you already use.

but they live in google drive and stream in 4 MiB chunks, so a 50 GB film takes no space on your mac.

turn on the optional ode1 vault and your files and filenames are end-to-end encrypted on your mac before they ever leave it.

2 why it’s different

traditional sync apps copy whole folders onto your ssd or trap your drive inside clunky localhost folders that report your local disk’s free space instead of your cloud quota.

open drive runs a user-space loopback nfsv3 and webdav engine over rfc 6761 *.localhost. finder shows your actual multi-terabyte google drive space, while a two-tier hot/cold lru cache coalesces 4 MiB byte-range reads on the fly.

3 how it works

  1. install open drive (or build from source in swift 6). it lives quietly in your menu bar.
  2. sign in with google oauth 2.0 pkce over loopback (127.0.0.1). tokens stay sealed in macos keychain.
  3. open your drive straight from finder favorites, rename it live, or unlock an encrypted ode1 vault.

questions, answered.

what is open drive?

an open-source macos cloud drive engineered in swift 6 that mounts your google drive directly in finder favorites and terminal. files look and open like local files, but stream on demand from google drive so they take zero space on your mac.

how does finder show my real 5 TB google drive space instead of my mac’s free space?

apple’s file provider framework reports your startup disk’s free space in finder. open drive includes a Virtual Disk mode powered by a user-space loopback nfsv3 and webdav server over rfc 6761 *.localhost that translates google drive’s about.get storageQuota directly into statfs / FSSTAT.

how can a large video open immediately if it isn’t on my mac?

open drive’s RangeReader fetches only the 4 MiB byte range your app requests, coalesces duplicate in-flight reads, and prefetches sequential chunks into a bounded two-tier hot/cold lru disk cache.

how does ode1 zero-knowledge encryption work?

when you enable the ode1 vault on an account, open drive derives a 256-bit key encryption key via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (210,000 iterations) to wrap your account master key in .od-meta. every file gets a 72-byte ODE1 header and random-access 4 MiB + 16 B AES-256-GCM chunks, while filenames are encrypted with HKDF-SHA256 (ODNames-v1).

what if i forget my vault passphrase?

when you create an encrypted vault, open drive generates a 24-word recovery phrase and a printable us-letter recovery key pdf. keep it somewhere safe — either your passphrase or the 24-word recovery key can unwrap your master key.

can i rename my mounted drive or connect multiple google accounts?

yes. you can rename the mounted volume live (which updates both the mount path under ~/Library/OpenDrive and your finder favorites pin) and connect multiple whole-drive or dedicated open drive folder accounts simultaneously.

does it work with terminal, git, symlinks, and executable scripts?

yes. open drive supports posix symlinks (ln -s), persists executable permission bits (chmod +x) across restarts, and isolates finder’s noisy ._* appledouble files in memory so they never clutter your remote google drive.

what are the system requirements?

a mac running macos 15.4 (sequoia) or newer, and your own free google cloud desktop oauth 2.0 client id (or build from source with xcode / swift 6 command line tools).
